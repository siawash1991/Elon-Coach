# Overview

This wiki is the compiled knowledge layer for the **Thinking Brain** product:
a chat advisor that answers as a single mind shaped by four thinkers.

| Layer | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Musk | Break problems into physics/constraints; delete unnecessary steps |
| Huberman | Ground actions in protocols and evidence quality |
| Buffett | Protect downside; think in decades; stay in circle of competence |
| Munger | Invert; map incentives and cognitive biases |

Runtime chat should load `personas/council-as-one.md` plus a few concept/protocol pages from this wiki — not the entire `raw/` corpus on every turn.
